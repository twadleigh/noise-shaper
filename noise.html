Engine starts with Play.

White Gaussian noise (ziggurat method, fresh every sample — nothing ever loops) is filtered by a 16,383-tap linear-phase FIR built from the curve, applied with FFT convolution. The result is exactly stationary filtered noise with no frames or seams in the signal. Output is normalized to a constant RMS level, and curve changes take effect within about a second through a level-matched crossfade.
